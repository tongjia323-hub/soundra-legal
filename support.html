How Soundra Works

  • Soundra uses the microphone for local real-time reference sound level analysis.
  • Audio is analyzed on the device and is not uploaded to developer servers.
  • Soundra is intended for recording preparation and everyday reference checks. It is not a certified legal, occupational, medical, or laboratory sound level meter.
