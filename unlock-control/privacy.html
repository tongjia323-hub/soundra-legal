01 · Scope

This policy covers the local web preview and iPhone / iPad development versions, including photo import, on-device text recognition, the development exercise counter and local StoreKit purchase tests. Features depend on the version used. Remote sync, cloud recognition and production store products are not enabled.

A parent or guardian arranges tasks. There is no online family account or remote identity verification. Protected parent actions in the local-data version require a six-digit parent PIN. A separate, labelled web demonstration uses fictional records and allows role switching without a PIN; it does not protect real family data.

02 · Parent PIN and local access

The parent sets a six-digit PIN on first setup. The app stores a random 32-byte salt and a 32-byte verification value derived with PBKDF2-SHA256 (210,000 iterations), not the PIN in plain text. It also stores failed-attempt counts and a waiting-period end time. These values are used only to verify local parent access and slow repeated guesses; they are not sent to developer servers.

On iPhone and iPad, this credential record is stored in the system Keychain with WhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly protection. In the web preview, it is stored in this site’s localStorage, protected by the browser and device. The web preview is not a secure device-management boundary: someone able to inspect or clear site storage can alter or remove its local records.

A successful verification opens a parent session in memory for up to five minutes. Leaving the app or web page for the background, entering a child task, or restarting requires verification again. Starting with the fifth failed attempt, another attempt must wait at least 60 seconds; repeated failures increase the wait, up to 15 minutes. A successful verification clears the failure count.

Complete initial setup privately and keep the parent PIN separate from the device passcode. In the native development version, Family → Change parent PIN requires the current PIN and two matching entries of a new six-digit PIN. It shares failed-attempt counts and waiting periods with sign-in. A successful change replaces the Keychain salt and verification value and ends the parent session; the new PIN is required afterward. There is still no forgotten-PIN reset or recovery, online account or cloud PIN sync. Support cannot retrieve or remotely reset the PIN. Deleting and reinstalling the native app is not a guaranteed reset because Keychain records can remain. The PIN does not replace the device passcode or Apple family authorization.

04 · Use and sharing

The current code does not send these task records to developer servers. It has no remote family sync and does not share this information with advertisers, data brokers or third-party analytics platforms. We do not sell children’s information.

Local information can still be personal data. Display names, assignments and free text may identify someone. Use nicknames and add only what a task needs. No developer upload does not mean that no personal data exists on your device.

05 · System permissions and files

The native version uses Apple Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity to request parental authorization and management of selected content. It uses opaque selection tokens, does not read a complete browsing history or contacts, and does not request precise location. Apple’s rules govern system processing; actual control capabilities depend on authorization and version.

Adding files in the web preview saves files you select in your local browser, not our servers. If you choose files from your own iCloud or another file service, that service and your actions govern downloading. Declining file selection does not import other files.

Parents may photograph or select worksheets, articles, word lists or sheet music, or enter content manually. Photos and recognized text create tasks, check limited simple arithmetic or support parental review, and stay on the current device. Native development supports photos and TXT / CSV; web attachment support differs. Photos are not uploaded to developer servers. Original files may include capture metadata.

06 · Reading, exercise and music practice

Exercise frames are processed only after the camera is started. Development logic supports squats, push-ups and sit-ups; accuracy is not validated. It does not grade form, identify faces or people, or prove rope passage. Frames are processed transiently on-device, without recording, storage or upload. Joint positions and counts provide live feedback. Submitted estimated counts may enter local task records and still require parental confirmation. Pausing, leaving the view or backgrounding the app stops the camera.

For music tasks with a practice timer enabled, the native development version stores the instrument, piece, passage or measures, target minutes, repetitions, tempo (BPM), written requirements and accumulated practice time on-device. Time is added only while the practice screen is active and the timer is running. Leaving the screen, locking the device or entering the background pauses it; saved elapsed time remains locally. Restarting does not add time spent away. If a parent asks for another attempt, that task’s practice timer starts over. These records do not prove that music was played: a parent checks the performance, repetitions, tempo and completion. The app does not use the microphone, record audio or automatically grade reading or musical performance.

07 · Retention, backups and protection

Saved task records and attachments remain until the relevant storage is cleared; there is no automatic expiry deletion, and reward expiry does not erase history. In the native development version, unfinished drafts remain after closing the editor, backgrounding or restarting. After PIN verification, a parent can discard an individual draft and its draft-photo copies. A successful task save attempts to remove its draft files; the saved task and any attached photo remain. If cleanup fails, draft files may remain; unreadable drafts are kept rather than silently overwritten. Drafts use iOS file protection and are not explicitly excluded from system backup. Source photos, backups and manual exports are managed separately.

There is no developer-operated cloud backup. Apple device backups, iCloud, system diagnostics or browser backups may retain copies according to your settings. Native data is not explicitly excluded from system backup. Manage backups, original files and manual exports separately.

The parent credential is kept separately from task files. Native Keychain data can remain after app deletion; the app currently provides no credential-deletion or recovery screen. Clearing this web preview’s site data removes its local credential and task storage. The Keychain protection is device-bound; this is not a promise that removing the app erases all system-held copies.

08 · Access, export, deletion and authorization

Parents can view local information in the task, history and settings pages available in their version. In the native development version, Drafts requires parent PIN verification to resume or discard an unfinished draft. Discarding a draft does not delete saved tasks, rewards or source photos. The web preview exports arrangements and records, including answer keys but excluding original attachments. Keep exports private. Full native export and individual deletion of saved tasks or reward history are not implemented.

Web: clearing this site’s data removes the local records, attachments and PIN credential; this is irreversible within the app. Native: “Delete App” removes the installation’s app data, while “Offload App” may retain it. Neither action is a guaranteed Keychain credential reset. The current app has no PIN reset or full credential-deletion control. Manage backups, original files and manual exports separately.

Parents can manage or revoke revocable permissions through the system’s Screen Time / family control settings; available steps depend on system version and authorization type. Revoking permission is separate from deleting records. We cannot remotely access, edit or delete information stored only on your device.

09 · Children and guardians

A parent or guardian selects the family tasks and content. The current versions do not ask children for a legal name, identity documents, email, phone number or precise location. There are no public child profiles, public posting or stranger chat features.

A guardian should make support requests involving a child. Do not submit a child’s face, voice, school, address or complete assignments unless needed and appropriately authorized. A guardian may contact the operator about accidentally submitted child information.

10 · When you contact support

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, request and any attachments you choose to provide, solely to respond, investigate and handle related legal obligations. Task records and children’s files are not attached automatically.

We retain support information only as long as needed to handle the request, necessary follow-up and applicable legal obligations. You may use the email below to request access, correction or deletion of support copies we hold. Gmail provides email services under Google’s Privacy Policy. Support cannot remotely delete device-only records.

13 · Purchases and restoration

Native development uses Apple StoreKit to verify a one-time full-unlock product, signed transactions, revocation and restoration. Only local test products are currently enabled; production store products are not. The app receives no payment-card details or Apple account passwords and has no developer payment server. Production purchases and restoration will be processed by Apple under its privacy policy. Access follows verified entitlements, not a locally toggled paid flag.
