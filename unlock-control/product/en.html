One routine. Different ways to practise.

01

Use your own material

Photograph words, a worksheet, an article or practice instructions. Text recognition runs on the device. Review and edit the result before using it; recognition may need correction.

02

Set a movement goal

Set an exercise target. Camera-assisted counting supports squats, push-ups and sit-ups only. Counts are a guide and may need checking; other exercises use parent review.

03

Give music practice a plan

Choose an instrument, piece, passage, minutes, repetitions and optional target tempo. After you start the timer, it counts only while the practice screen is open in the foreground. Leaving the screen, locking the device or backgrounding the app pauses it. A parent confirms completion. The app does not listen to or score the performance.

04

Parents set the rules

Word tasks and supported basic math questions are checked against the answers you set. Review other tasks, send them back with feedback, and protect parent actions with a local six-digit PIN. Set a daily reward limit and a rest time.

FULL UNLOCK

One-time unlock

A single in-app purchase unlocks Math, Reading aloud, Exercise, Music practice and Custom tasks. No monthly or yearly subscription. Your local price appears on the purchase screen.

A few things to know.

SCREEN TIME, WITH APPLE AUTHORIZATION

On a supported child's device, complete Apple's family setup and authorize Screen Time to select the apps, categories and websites to restrict. Task rewards can be used to request entertainment time. This does not replace the device passcode or lock the whole device.

Local by design

Task content, text recognition and exercise-camera processing stay on the device. No app account is required. This is a local workflow, without remote task delivery or cloud sync.
