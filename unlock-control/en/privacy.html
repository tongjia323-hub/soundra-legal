01 · Scope

SHENGJIA TONG operates Unlock Control. This policy covers version 1.0 on iPhone and iPad: parent-created tasks, photo and TXT / CSV import, on-device text recognition, exercise counting assistance, practice timing, authorized device restrictions, and Apple in-app purchases.

A parent or guardian arranges tasks and reviews completion. There is no online family account, remote family linking, cloud recognition or developer-operated synchronization. A six-digit parent PIN protects parent actions on this device; it does not replace a device passcode or Apple's family authorization.

02 · Parent PIN and local access

On first setup, the parent creates a six-digit PIN. The app stores a random 32-byte salt and a 32-byte verification value derived with PBKDF2-SHA256 (210,000 iterations), not the plain-text PIN. Failed-attempt counts and a waiting-period end time help slow repeated guesses. These values are not sent to developer servers.

The credential record is stored in the system Keychain with WhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly protection. The parent session exists only in memory and locks when the app leaves the foreground or a child task opens. Device access and Apple's system protections still matter.

To change the PIN, enter the current PIN and the new six-digit PIN twice in Family → Change parent PIN. An unlocked session does not skip the current-PIN check. Wrong attempts share the sign-in waiting period; a successful change locks the parent session. Forgotten-PIN recovery, remote reset and full credential deletion are not available. Reinstalling the app may retain the Keychain record.

04 · Use and sharing

Task records, photos, recognized text and device-control state are processed on this device and are not uploaded to developer servers. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs, advertising tracking, data-broker sharing or sale of children's information.

Local records may still contain personal information, such as names or details in assignments and free text. Use nicknames where practical and add only the material a task needs. Apple services, device backups, external websites and voluntary support email have their own processing described below.

05 · System permissions and files

Apple Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity are used for authorized restrictions on selected apps, categories and websites. The app and its extension share opaque selection tokens, rules, reward budgets and callback state in a local App Group container. The app does not read a complete browsing history or contacts, and does not request precise location. System controls depend on Apple's authorization and the device configuration.

Parents can take or select photos of worksheets, articles, word lists or sheet music, or choose TXT / CSV files. Photos, recognized text and task attachments stay on the current device; selecting a file does not import other files. A file chosen from iCloud or another file provider may be downloaded by that provider under its own settings. Original files may contain capture metadata.

06 · Reading, exercise and music practice

Exercise counting uses camera frames and Apple Vision on the device after the camera is started. Squats, push-ups and sit-ups have counting assistance; estimates require parent review and accuracy is not guaranteed. It does not identify faces or people, grade form, or verify a rope passing under the feet. Camera frames and joint positions are processed transiently without recording, storage or developer upload. Submitted estimated counts may be saved in local task records. Pausing, leaving the view or backgrounding the app stops the camera.

Music plans and practice time are stored locally. Time increases only while the practice view is active and its timer is running; leaving the view, locking the device or backgrounding pauses it. Saved time persists, while time away is not added after restarting. A new attempt requested by a parent resets that task's practice timer. A timer does not prove that music was played. A parent checks performance, repetitions, tempo and completion. The app does not use the microphone, record audio or automatically score music or reading.

07 · Retention, backups and protection

Saved tasks, attachments and rewards remain until the relevant app storage is removed; reward expiry does not delete history. Unfinished drafts remain after closing the editor or restarting. A parent can discard a draft and its draft-photo copies after PIN verification. Saving a task attempts to remove its draft files, but the saved task and attachment remain. Cleanup failures can leave draft files; unreadable drafts are retained rather than silently overwritten.

Local files use iOS file protection. Native data is not explicitly excluded from system backups. There is no developer-operated cloud backup. Apple device backups, iCloud and system diagnostics follow the device settings and Apple's policies. Manage original files, external copies and backups separately.

The PIN credential is separate from task files and can remain in Keychain after app deletion. Delete App removes installation data; Offload App may retain it. Neither is a guaranteed PIN reset or deletion of all system-held copies.

08 · Access, export, deletion and authorization

Parents can view local tasks, completion records, rewards and settings in the app. Open Drafts after PIN verification to resume or discard an unfinished draft. Discarding a draft does not delete saved tasks, rewards, original photos or backups. Full native export and individual deletion of saved tasks or reward history are not available in version 1.0; keep original worksheets, scores and files separately.

Use the device's settings to manage revocable permissions; available steps depend on the system version and authorization type. Revoking a permission is separate from deleting records. Support cannot access, restore, edit or remotely delete device-only information. For support information you voluntarily sent, use the email below to request access, correction or deletion.

09 · Children and guardians

A parent or guardian chooses the tasks and materials. The app does not ask children for a legal name, identity documents, email address, phone number or precise location. It has no public child profiles, public posts or stranger chat.

Support requests involving a child should come from a parent or guardian. Remove names, faces, school details and unrelated personal information from screenshots and attachments. Do not send passwords, Apple ID verification codes or a full family dataset. Contact the operator about accidentally submitted child information.

13 · Purchases and restoration

The app is free to download and the complete word-task flow is free. Other task types are unlocked through one non-consumable purchase. The configured US price is US$3.99; Apple shows the actual localized price before purchase. This is not a subscription and does not renew automatically.

Apple StoreKit processes purchases and restoration. The app checks signed transactions and entitlements on the device, including revocation; it has no developer payment or receipt server and receives no payment-card details or Apple account passwords. Access follows verified Apple entitlements rather than a locally toggled paid flag.

Use Restore purchases with the Apple account used for the purchase. Availability, purchase authorization and refunds follow Apple's processes.
