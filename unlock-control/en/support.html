Frequently asked questions

Are my files sent to your servers?

No tasks or attachments are automatically sent to developer servers. They remain on this device. Apple purchases, system backups, external file providers and email you choose to send are separate processes described in the privacy policy.

Does 40 minutes of practice automatically receive a score?

No. The app records foreground practice time, not proof of playing or a musical score. A parent reviews the performance, repetitions, tempo and completion. There is no microphone recording or automatic music or reading assessment. Exercise counts are estimates for parent review.

How can I keep a copy?

Version 1.0 has no full native export. Keep original worksheets, scores and files separately and manage device backups in the system settings.

How do I delete records?

After parent PIN verification, open Drafts, choose Discard draft and confirm. This removes that draft and its draft-photo copies, not saved tasks, rewards, original photos or backups. Individual deletion of saved tasks and reward history is not available. Delete App removes installation data, while Offload App may retain it. Neither guarantees that a Keychain PIN credential is reset.

Are parent and child records protected?

A six-digit parent PIN protects task setup, review and rule changes. The session locks when leaving the foreground or entering a child task. It does not replace the device passcode or Apple's family authorization.

I forgot the parent PIN. Can support reset it?

No. There is no online account, forgotten-PIN recovery or remote reset. Support cannot retrieve or reset the PIN or restore device-only records. Reinstalling the app may retain the Keychain credential.

How do I change a parent PIN I still know?

Open Family → Change parent PIN. Enter the current PIN, then the new six-digit PIN twice. An unlocked parent session does not skip this check. Wrong attempts share the sign-in waiting period. After a successful change, use the new PIN to enter parent mode.

Where is my unfinished task?

Open Drafts after PIN verification to resume or discard it. Drafts keep task inputs, answer keys, music fields and imported photos or recognized text locally. A draft earns no reward. If an import was interrupted, choose the photo again or cancel the unfinished import. Saving a task attempts to clear its draft; cleanup failures may leave draft files. There is no remote draft synchronization.

How is the app paid for?

Download and the complete word-task flow are free. One non-consumable purchase unlocks the other task types; the configured US price is US$3.99, with the actual local price shown by Apple. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. Use Restore purchases with the Apple account used to buy it.

Include in a support request

  • App version and device model.
  • Steps, time and the error text.
  • Redact children’s names, answers and private information from screenshots.
  • Do not send passwords, Apple ID verification codes or a full family dataset.

Deletion requests

Support cannot access device-only records. If you sent information to us, contact tongjia323@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of support copies we hold.
