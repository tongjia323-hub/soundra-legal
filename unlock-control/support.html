Frequently asked questions

Are my files sent to your servers?

The current versions do not automatically upload tasks or attachments. Web imports stay in the local browser. Manual exports, emails and system backups depend on you or the relevant system.

Does 40 minutes of practice automatically receive a score?

For music tasks with a practice timer enabled, the native development version stores the instrument, piece, passage or measures, target minutes, repetitions, tempo (BPM), written requirements and accumulated practice time on-device. Time is added only while the practice screen is active and the timer is running. Leaving the screen, locking the device or entering the background pauses it; saved elapsed time remains locally. Restarting does not add time spent away. If a parent asks for another attempt, that task’s practice timer starts over. These records do not prove that music was played: a parent checks the performance, repetitions, tempo and completion. The app does not use the microphone, record audio or automatically grade reading or musical performance. Exercise frames are processed only after the camera is started. Development logic supports squats, push-ups and sit-ups; accuracy is not validated. It does not grade form, identify faces or people, or prove rope passage. Frames are processed transiently on-device, without recording, storage or upload. Joint positions and counts provide live feedback. Submitted estimated counts may enter local task records and still require parental confirmation. Pausing, leaving the view or backgrounding the app stops the camera.

How can I keep a copy?

Web settings allow record export, including answer keys but not original attachments. The native validation version has no full export. Keep original worksheets, scores and files separately.

How do I delete records?

Web: clearing this site’s data removes the local records, attachments and PIN credential; this is irreversible within the app. Native: “Delete App” removes the installation’s app data, while “Offload App” may retain it. Neither action is a guaranteed Keychain credential reset. The current app has no PIN reset or full credential-deletion control. Manage backups, original files and manual exports separately.

Are parent and child records protected?

A six-digit parent PIN protects parent actions in the local-data version, and the session expires or locks when leaving the app or entering a child task. It is not a substitute for a device passcode or Apple family authorization. The fictional web demonstration bypasses the PIN. Browser storage can be inspected or cleared by someone with device access.

I forgot the parent PIN. Can support reset it?

No. There is no online account, PIN recovery or reset feature. Support cannot retrieve or remotely reset your local PIN. Reinstalling the native app may retain the Keychain credential. Clearing web site data removes the preview’s PIN together with its locally stored records; we cannot restore that data.

Include in a support request

  • App version and device model.
  • Steps, time and the error text.
  • Redact children’s names, answers and private information from screenshots.
  • Do not send passwords, Apple ID verification codes or a full family dataset.

Deletion requests

Support cannot access device-only records. If you sent information to us, contact tongjia323@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of support copies we hold.
