Forma Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-05-13

Forma is a local video conversion utility. User videos are processed on device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Data Collection

Forma V1 does not collect personal data. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location, photo library contents, video content, audio content, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, or converted files.

Video Processing

Selected videos are used locally to inspect media information and perform conversion. File names, sizes, duration, formats, codecs, and resolution are shown only inside the app and are not sent to a developer server.

Purchases

Forma uses an on-device 3-day trial that starts on first launch. Lifetime purchase and restore purchases are handled by Apple In-App Purchase. Forma does not use auto-renewable subscriptions.

Network and Servers

Forma V1 does not provide accounts, cloud storage, cloud sync, cloud transcoding, social features, messaging, online content, or developer-operated internet information services.

Third Parties

Forma V1 does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.
