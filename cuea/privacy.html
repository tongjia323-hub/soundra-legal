Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

English

Cuea is a local teleprompter app. Cuea V1 does not provide accounts, cloud sync, social features, user content publishing, cloud processing, or developer-operated upload services.

Cuea does not collect data for tracking, does not use advertising tracking, does not include third-party analytics SDKs, and does not upload user scripts, recordings, videos, camera frames, or microphone detection data to developer servers.

Data Handling

  • Scripts: scripts created, pasted, or imported by the user are stored locally on device.
  • Microphone: used for recording and local pause detection. Cuea does not upload microphone data, does not perform word-by-word speech tracking, and does not send audio to developer servers.
  • Camera: used for recording and monitoring when the user starts camera mode.
  • Photo Library: used only when the user saves a recorded video.
  • File Import: TXT, RTF, DOC, and DOCX files are processed locally on device.
  • Purchases: Cuea Pro purchase and restore flows are handled by Apple StoreKit and the App Store. Cuea does not upload scripts, recordings, videos, camera frames, or microphone detection data for purchase or restore.

Trial and In-App Purchase

Cuea Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock. The in-app 3-day Pro trial is recorded locally on device. The trial start time is stored in UserDefaults and Keychain only to determine whether the trial has started and whether it has expired. This record is not uploaded to developer servers.

中文

Cuea 是一个本地提词器 App。Cuea V1 不提供账号系统、云同步、社交、用户内容发布、云处理或开发者服务器上传功能。

Cuea 不收集可用于追踪用户的数据，不接入广告追踪，不接入第三方分析 SDK，不向开发者服务器上传用户脚本、录音、视频、相机画面或麦克风检测数据。

数据处理

  • 脚本：用户创建、粘贴或导入的脚本保存在设备本地。
  • 麦克风：用于录音和本机停顿检测。Cuea 不上传麦克风数据，不做逐字语音识别，不把音频发送到开发者服务器。
  • 相机：用于用户主动开启的录制和监看。
  • 相册：仅在用户主动保存视频时写入系统相册。
  • 文件导入：TXT、RTF、DOC、DOCX 文件在设备本机处理。
  • 购买：Cuea Pro 购买和恢复购买通过 Apple StoreKit 与 App Store 完成。Cuea 不借此上传稿件、录音、视频、相机画面或麦克风检测数据。

试用和内购

Cuea Pro 是一次性买断内购。App 内 3 天 Pro 试用由设备本机记录，试用开始时间保存在 UserDefaults 和 Keychain 中，用于判断试用是否已经开启和是否到期。该记录不上传到开发者服务器。
