What Cuea Does

  • Script management and local script editing.
  • Voice Follow prompting: text moves when you speak and holds when you pause.
  • Camera recording with microphone audio and a separate on-screen prompt layer.
  • TXT, RTF, DOC, and DOCX import.
  • Portrait, landscape, iPhone, and iPad support.

Privacy and Local Processing

Cuea does not provide accounts, cloud sync, social publishing, or developer-operated upload services. Scripts, camera preview, microphone activity, and recordings stay on the user's device by default.

In-App Purchase

Cuea Pro is a non-consumable one-time lifetime unlock. The in-app 3-day trial is handled locally by the app and is not an auto-renewable subscription.
