# 烟账使用条款

状态：正式发布。

## 中文

烟账是一款面向成年用户的戒烟减量与烟草使用成本记录工具。

### 使用边界

烟账用于帮助用户记录烟草使用成本、延迟一次冲动、记录未点着的烟，并将减少使用部分换算为避免支出金额。

烟账不销售、不推荐、不推广任何烟草产品，也不鼓励用户使用烟草。

### 非医疗声明

烟账不提供医疗建议、诊断或治疗服务。App 内的记录、统计和提醒仅用于个人自我管理参考。

如果你需要戒烟医疗建议、健康评估或治疗方案，请咨询合格医疗专业人员。

### 避免支出说明

烟账记录的是避免支出金额，并不代表实际入账、投资收益或金融账户余额。

### 本地数据

V1.0 默认在用户设备本机保存数据。不提供账号、社交、云同步或用户内容上传。

## English

Yanzhang is a smoking reduction and tobacco use cost tracking tool for adult users.

### Scope of Use

Yanzhang helps users track tobacco use cost, delay an urge, record cigarettes not lit, and convert reduced use into avoided spending.

Yanzhang does not sell, recommend, or promote any tobacco product, and does not encourage tobacco use.

### No Medical Advice

Yanzhang does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Records, statistics, and reminders in the app are for personal self-management reference only.

If you need medical advice, health assessment, or treatment for quitting smoking, please consult a qualified medical professional.

### Avoided Spending

Yanzhang records avoided spending. It does not represent actual income, investment return, or financial account balance.

### Local Data

Version 1.0 stores data locally on the user's device by default. It does not provide accounts, social features, cloud sync, or user content upload.
